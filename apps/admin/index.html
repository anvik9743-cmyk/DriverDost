🚀 5 Steps to Activate Razorpay Live Payments
Follow these 5 steps to verify your business and connect your merchant bank account:
1
Create Razorpay Account: Sign up with your email or phone number at razorpay.com. Enter your business name and website URL.
2
Choose Business Type: Select either Individual or Sole Proprietorship and enter your PAN details. Most details auto-fill directly from your PAN.
3
Complete KYC: Verify the auto-filled name and address details from your PAN to complete the Know Your Customer verification.
4
Payment Channels & Bank Details: Select Website & App payment channels, then enter your bank account & IFSC for automated T+1 settlement payouts.
5
Generate API Keys: Once approved, visit Settings → API Keys in Razorpay Dashboard. Generate your Key ID and Key Secret, paste them on the left, and start accepting live customer payments!
